§03

Eleven decisions, resolved with you

The designs disagreed with the confirmed stack, or with themselves, in four places that reshaped the architecture. Two follow-up discussions on 21 Aug 2026 then escalated three of those calls to a more rigorous position — the best approach rather than the fastest — and added a whole new decision area: how each School gets its own branded mobile app. Nothing below was assumed — each was a direct question, answered in this session.

Decided

Payment scope

Stripe (cards) plus manually-recorded Cash and Bank Transfer. GoCardless and PayPal — both fully designed — are out of scope for this build.

Decided · revised 21 Aug

App boundary

School Portal and Platform Admin are two separately deployed React apps in one monorepo from day one, each with its own subdomain, deployment, and identity realm — not a single codebase with role-gated views. Full detail in §04.

Decided

Canonical screen set

Where a feature was designed twice, the newer KPI/dashboard-driven, card-based version is the source of truth — not the older plain CRUD tables.

Decided

Tenancy hierarchy

Franchise → Schools → Branches, with a School's franchise link optional — independent, non-franchised schools are fully supported.

New · 21 Aug

Payment-credential custody

Move to Stripe Connect as the primary model, with a secrets manager as fallback — ULTM8 never custodies a tenant's raw, decryptable Stripe secret key. See §04 and §07.

New · 21 Aug

Tenant isolation

Enforced at the database via PostgreSQL Row-Level Security, backed by a required CI test suite that asserts cross-tenant access is impossible before any merge. Full detail in §04.

New · 21 Aug

Platform Admin identity

A separate identity realm from customer accounts — SSO, mandatory 2FA, and least-privilege sub-roles instead of one Admin bucket. Full detail in §04.

New · 21 Aug

White-label app model

Every School ships inside one shared "ULTM8" app by default; a paid plan upgrade adds the School's own branded app on both stores. Full detail in §05.

New · 21 Aug

White-label publish ownership

ULTM8 requests admin access to each School's Apple/Google accounts and runs the build-and-submit pipeline on the School's behalf — Schools don't self-serve releases. Full detail in §05.

New · 21 Aug

White-label downgrade policy

A branded app is frozen at its last build on downgrade or cancellation, not unpublished — it stays live and keeps working against the API. Full detail in §05.

New · 21 Aug

White-label build timing

The publishing pipeline is built alongside the initial platform, not deferred until the first paying upgrade, so it's a feature Schools hear about from launch. Full detail in §05.

§04

Why Platform Admin gets its own app

A single role-gated app was the faster build, but it means one leaked customer JWT, one forgotten @Roles() check, or one over-broad query is all that stands between a support ticket and every tenant's data. You asked for the approach that avoids scaling problems later, not the quickest one now — so this supersedes §03's original App Boundary call.

§05

How each School gets its own app

Code Studio's direction: once the platform is live, a School can have its own branded app — own name, own icon — listed separately on the App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google both regulate this directly (an app built from a template is rejected unless the business itself is the publisher of record), so it's modeled as a gated upgrade, not a toggle.

§06

The system we're proposing

Three clients — two separately-deployed React web apps (§04) plus the React Native student app — share one API, with Stripe kept off the request/response path: its webhooks land on the same queue that already handles OTP and notifications, so a slow or retried Stripe event never blocks a user-facing call.

§08

Where the designs run out

Nothing here was quietly decided for you. Each item is either undesigned, contradictory, or a technical convention the confirmed stack doesn't cover.

Needs a provider or convention decision

  • Object storage / CDN — for banners, galleries, profile photos, signed waivers and invoice PDFs. Not part of the confirmed stack.
  • SMS, push, and email providers — every OTP and Notification screen implies delivery; none is specified.
  • ORM and API style — Prisma vs. TypeORM, REST vs. GraphQL — neither is fixed by the stack.
  • Environments & CI/CD — no dev/staging/production or hosting strategy exists anywhere in scope.
  • Stripe Connect onboarding fit — §04 commits to Connect as the primary custody model; still to confirm is whether Express vs. Standard accounts and per-country availability fit every market, and which flows genuinely need the secrets-manager fallback.
  • White-label add-on pricing — §05 decides the branded-app upgrade is a recurring plan add-on, sized to cover build/submission cost and each School's own store fees, but the actual price point isn't set.
  • Shared-app store approval — the included, shared Student app needs to pass App Review as a legitimate aggregator/directory app itself, not a template shell; recommend a pre-submission consultation with Apple before the first branded upgrade goes live.

Needs product clarification

  • Passcode as credential — is the 6-digit passcode the entire login, or a PIN over a longer password not shown?
  • Franchise Subscription Plans — platform billing, or a plan the franchise resells to its own schools?
  • "User" role — the translations CMS groups content under Student / School / Admin / User, which maps to nothing else in the designs.
  • No instructor login — instructors exist only as records; confirm whether they need their own access.
  • Unlabeled profile stats — four unexplained numbers (870 / 120k / 354k / 3.8Gb) on the Student profile.
  • Currency conversion — unclear whether the 6 supported currencies convert automatically or are entered independently.

Sidebar items with no screen behind them

  • QR Code, Notification, Rules, Subscription History, Reports, Customer Services — all appear in the School Portal navigation; none has a corresponding designed screen.
  • Two mobile bottom-nav layouts — most screens vs. Coach Profile/video screens disagree on what belongs in the tab bar.
  • Likely copy/paste artifacts — a duplicated "Microsoft" login button, and near-duplicate frames like createSchoolProfi / createSchoolProfile.
