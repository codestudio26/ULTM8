## 1. What Version 1.3 is

Version 1.3 is the **new look of the whole ULTM8 site**, plus the backend features the new screens need.

- **Frontend (designed, being built on the redesign branch):**
  - a new side menu
  - white pages with a details panel on the right
  - **one form style ("F") on every form across the site**
  - a phone-friendly layout of the same design (§7)
- **Backend (this document):** the new screens show features that ULTM8's backend doesn't support yet. Each one appears on screen as a grey **"Soon"** tag or a disabled control, never with made-up data. This document lists every one of them, with as much explanation as possible, so the team can build them.

The product owner's direction is that ULTM8 should **match as many functions of Gymdesk (its main competitor) as possible**. Most items come from a screen-by-screen comparison with Gymdesk. The IDs in brackets (M6, PR7, CC1 …) point to that comparison.

## 2. Ground rules (please read first)

1. **Spec 55 and the project rules still apply.** The source-of-truth order in `CLAUDE.md` stands:
   - Spec 55 comes first, then `skills/ultm8-domain-rules/SKILL.md`, then the decision log.
   - Grading follows Gus's prototype rules (Decision 124 onward).
   - **Tenancy and Row-Level Security** (Franchise → School → Branch) must hold for every new table, endpoint and job. Every new School-owned table carries `schoolId` and an RLS policy, like the existing ones.
2. **Tags:**
   - `[CONFIRMED]`: the business rules are decided. Design and build.
   - `[UNRESOLVED]`: an open question. **Don't build until it's answered.** Ask the product owner; never fill the gap with a guess.
   - `[DEFERRED]`: parked on purpose.
3. **⚠️ NEEDS A DECISION ENTRY:** the item changes a rule that Spec 55 or an earlier decision fixed.
   - Before building, add a decision to `docs/decisions/POST-SPEC-55-DECISION-LOG.md` under the next free number, quoting the product owner's answer recorded here.
   - The answers aren't in the log yet, because the redesign branch can't take decision numbers without colliding with `master`.
4. **Children (minors) and Guardians:** every item touching a minor follows the Guardian consent model (SKILL.md §14, Decision 236). When in doubt, the Guardian acts for the child.
5. **Defaults change nothing.** Every new School setting defaults to exactly what ULTM8 does today. A School that never touches it sees no change.
6. **The live app must not change** until the product owner switches the new look on.
   - Backend work can merge to `master` normally, as long as it is **additive**: new fields optional or defaulted, new endpoints, and no change to existing responses.
   - The new screens start calling it when they go live.
7. **How to read "Suggested build":** this is a suggestion of the likely shape, written to save the team time. **The team designs the final shape.** Where a suggestion conflicts with Spec 55, Spec 55 wins.
8. **"Done when"** lists checks taken from the product owner's confirmed rules. Treat them as acceptance criteria.
9. Facts about existing code were checked against `apps/api/prisma/schema.prisma` and the decision log on 20 Oct 2026. Re-check before relying on them.

### 3.1 Visitor status (M6, Q6) ⚠️ NEEDS A DECISION ENTRY

**Where it shows:**
- **Add student:** a **Student / Visitor** switch at the top of the form.
- **Students list:** a **Visitors** filter chip and an orange **Visitor** badge.
- **Student panel:** "Visitor pass · ends 27 Oct".
- **Settings → Check-in rules:** "Allow visitors to check in during their visitor period".

**What it must do (product owner, 20 Oct 2026):**
- A Visitor is **not a member of the School but is allowed to use the facilities**.
- A Visitor has **its own status**, separate from Trial Membership.
  - Trial Membership keeps its platform caps unchanged: max 30 days per trial and 30 days in any rolling 12 months (SKILL.md §6, Decisions 54/64).
  - The Visitor status has **no platform limit**.
- The **visitor period** is set per Visitor by the **account owner, or staff with permission**. It can be **any length** (for example 1 day or 1 week), and when it **ends, the visitor status expires**.
- A Visitor follows **the same process as members**: signs the waiver, books classes, checks in.
- A Visitor **can buy a membership**, and becomes a member when they do.

**Suggested build:**
- **Data:**
  - A person-level status at the School, for example `VisitorPass { schoolId, userId, startsAt, endsAt, createdById }`, or fields on the existing School–student link.
  - "Is a Visitor" = an unexpired visitor pass and no active Membership.
  - Keep it **separate** from `MembershipPlan` TRIAL so the Trial caps aren't affected.
- **Endpoints:**
  - create or extend a visitor pass
  - end it early
  - list Visitors (for the Students list filter and count)
- **Rules and jobs:**
  - Expiry is a date check, computed live, the same approach Decision 26 uses for membership expiry. A batch flip isn't needed.
  - Optionally, a reminder before the pass ends; the product owner hasn't asked for one yet.
  - Booking and check-in must accept a valid visitor pass where they currently require an active Membership. This is gated by the School's check-in rule in §3.5.
  - Buying a membership ends the visitor status.

**Done when:**
- A Visitor with a valid pass can sign the waiver, book and check in.
- After `endsAt` they can't.
- Trial Membership limits are unchanged.
- The Students list can filter Visitors.

**Still to decide** `[UNRESOLVED]`:
- A child Visitor presumably needs a Guardian, as for any minor. Confirm.
- Which staff permission may set the visitor period?
- Can a Visitor have more than one pass in a row?

### 3.2 Personal check-in code (M7, Q7)

**Where it shows:**
- **Add student:** "Personal check-in code".
- **Student panel and profile:** a dashed "ticket" with the code and a QR icon.
- **Front-desk check-in screen:** the **Enter code** keypad.
- **Settings → Check-in rules:** "Personal check-in code length".
- **Membership card (§3.11):** the code is printed on it.

**What it must do (20 Oct 2026):**
- Every student has a **personal check-in code**, a **backup to the QR code** for when the QR doesn't work or the student has no phone.
- Typing it on the front-desk keypad checks the student in, under the School's check-in rules (§3.5).

**How it differs from today:** today's 6-digit code (Decision 235, `CheckInMethod` "type the code the class screen shows") changes **per class**. This new code belongs to the **person** and stays the same.

**Suggested build:**
- **Data:** a code per student per School, stored hashed or plain as the team judges. It's a low-value secret, like a gym PIN.
- **Endpoints:**
  - "Check in by personal code at this School/Branch now". This finds the student's booking for the current class, then records the check-in on the Booking, as today (`Booking.checkInMethod`).
  - It probably needs a new `CheckInMethod` value, for example `PERSONAL_CODE`, so reports can tell it apart.
  - Regenerate code.
- **Security:** rate-limit attempts per device and Branch, so codes can't be guessed. Log failures.

**Done when:**
- A student can check in at the front desk with their code.
- The check-in is recorded with its own method.
- Repeated wrong codes are slowed down.

**Decided** (Decision 315, 25 Oct 2026): generated by ULTM8; 4 or 6 digits, chosen by the School (4 by default); unique within the School; a Guardian can check a child in with the child's code; staff can issue a new one; the student can't change it.

### 3.4 Freezing a membership (G3, Q25) ⚠️ NEEDS A DECISION ENTRY

**Where it shows:**
- **Students list:** a **Frozen** filter chip and a blue **Frozen** badge.
- **Student panel:** "Monthly Unlimited · frozen".
- **Profile → Memberships:** a **Freeze** button (Soon).
- **Settings → Check-in rules:** "Allow frozen members to check in".
- **Billing column:** "Paused".

**What it must do (20 Oct 2026):**
- A membership **can be frozen**.
- **Each School sets the maximum freeze length.**
- The School **may charge a freeze fee**.
- **Billing stops** while frozen.
- **For a child, the Guardian requests** the freeze.

**Why it needs a decision entry:** `enum MembershipStatus` has only `ACTIVE` and `EXPIRED` (Decision 6). Expiry is computed live by `MembershipsService.getMembershipStatus()` (Decision 26).

**Suggested build:**
- **Data:**
  - A `MembershipFreeze { membershipId, schoolId, startsAt, endsAt, requestedById, approvedById?, feeAmount? }` table, rather than a stored status.
  - "Frozen" = today falls inside a freeze. This keeps Decision 26's live-computed approach.
  - `getMembershipStatus()` learns a frozen result.
- **School settings:** max freeze length, and a freeze fee (amount, optional).
- **Billing:**
  - Pause the Stripe subscription for the freeze period. Stripe supports pausing collection.
  - Work out how `billingCycleAnchorDay` and the next charge date move when the freeze ends (Decision 259's billing-cycle strategy).
  - Charge the freeze fee through the normal payment path, or onto the member balance (§3.6) for cash payers.
- **Expiry:** decide whether a frozen membership's `expiryDate` is pushed back by the freeze length. This is common, but confirm with the product owner.
- **Guardian flow:**
  - A request endpoint for the Guardian (student app).
  - Staff can also freeze directly.

**Done when:**
- A membership can be frozen up to the School's maximum.
- No charges happen during the freeze.
- The fee, if set, is charged once.
- The status shows as frozen everywhere.
- A child's freeze can only be requested by their Guardian.

**Still to decide** `[UNRESOLVED]`:
- Does access (booking and check-in) stop too? The check-in setting in §3.5 suggests each School chooses.
- Who approves a freeze request?
- Does the expiry date move?
- Can a freeze be ended early?

### 3.17 Remove decision numbers from text people see (found 20 Oct 2026)

> **29 Oct 2026: built.** All error messages and history notes the API sends are plain text now, with the reference kept in a code comment above each. Migration `20261225000000_clean_system_notes` cleans saved notes, and `apps/api/src/translations/no-decision-numbers.spec.ts` stops new ones. The translated messages in `packages/i18n` and the portal's "free text" hint were cleaned too. Log lines and API docs keep their numbers.

**The problem:**
- Some texts the API sends to the screen end with an internal reference, e.g. "Self-declared rank corrected while verifying **(Decision 147)**."
- These numbers are for the team, not for schools or students, and they look broken to them.
- The portal shows history notes and error messages exactly as the API sends them, so the numbers reach the screen.

**Where (on `master`, 20 Oct 2026):**
- **History notes, saved with each grading entry** (`PromotionEvent.systemNote`), in `apps/api/src/ranks/grading.service.ts`:
  - line 941: "Promoted in a batch; acknowledged: … (Decision 130)."
  - line 1713: "Declared when joining: the plain first belt, verified automatically (Decision 147)."
  - line 1714: "Declared when joining: waiting to be verified by the School (Decision 137)."
  - line 1798: "Self-declared rank corrected while verifying (Decision 147)."
- **Error messages shown in grading dialogs**, same file:
  - lines 624, 627, 659, 678, 776, 780, 915, 932, 1263, 1267 and 1578 (Decisions 128, 130, 166, 174, 176).
- **Other error messages:**
  - `ranks/ranks.service.ts:431` (Decision 180)
  - `auth/strategies/jwt.strategy.ts:67` and `:86` (Decisions 102 and 123)
  - `platform-admin/platform-admin-impersonation.service.ts:87` (Decision 102)
- **Not part of this:** descriptions in the API docs (Swagger `@ApiProperty` text). Developers read those, and they can keep the numbers.

**What it must do:** every text a person can see reads as plain English with no "(Decision N)". The rules behind them don't change.

**Suggested build:**
1. **Change the texts.**
   - Drop the "(Decision N)" / "(Decision N, item M)" part from each string listed above.
   - Keep the reference as a code comment on the line above, so the trail back to the decision log isn't lost.
   - Example:
     - before: `systemNote: 'Self-declared rank corrected while verifying (Decision 147).'`
     - after: `// Decision 147` on the line above, then `systemNote: 'Self-declared rank corrected while verifying.'`
2. **Clean the notes already saved.**
   - History notes are stored in the database, so old entries keep the number until they're fixed.

### 3.19 Ranks without a belt: Levels for No-Gi (product owner, 21 Oct 2026) ⚠️ NEEDS A DECISION ENTRY

**Where it shows:** Grading → Styles & belts (the "No-Gi" sample style), the belt editor, and every place a belt picture appears. See DESIGN.md → Grading screens → "Ranks without a belt".

**What the product owner decided (21 Oct 2026):**
- A style shows its ranks either as **Belts** (today) or as **Levels (no belt)**, never a mix.
- A level is shown as a **coloured badge**. The school picks the colour.
- A level has **numbered steps** (Beginner 1, 2, 3), each with its own requirements, like stripe grades.

**What it must do:**
- Grading itself doesn't change: steps are rungs, and requirements, progress, the board, promote, bulk and history all work exactly as for stripes.

**Suggested build:**
- **`Discipline.rankDisplay`:** an enum `BELTS | LEVELS`, default `BELTS`.
  - Set it on create.
  - Changing it while students hold ranks is allowed, because it's display only.
- **For a `LEVELS` style:**
  - `Rank.primaryColour` is the badge colour.
  - `secondaryColour` and `tagColour` stay null.
  - Each step is a `RankStripeTier` with empty `stripeSegments`; its default name is "{level} {n}".
  - Reject stripe segments on a `LEVELS` style with a 400.
- **API:** return `rankDisplay` on every discipline/ladder response, so the portal and the student app can draw a badge instead of a belt.
- **Templates:** the three IBJJF templates stay `BELTS`. A "Levels" style is built from scratch or duplicated.
- **Validation messages** become rank-neutral where they mention belts, e.g. "Pick a base colour." becomes "Pick a colour."

**Done when:**
- A style can be created as Levels.
- Its ranks and steps save without stripes.
- Every screen that shows a belt shows the badge for that style, in the portal and the student app.

**Where it shows:** a **Front-desk mode** button on Students → Check-in opens a full-screen, locked mode. It has today's classes in a grey column, a **Scan · Code · Name** switch, **New here? Sign up** and **Exit**. The design ("Calm split", 27 Oct 2026), with its tablet-sideways, tablet-upright and phone sizes, is in `DESIGN.md` → "Check-in and front-desk mode". The Gymdesk comparison is in `docs/design-references/gymdesk/GYMDESK-PARITY-BACKLOG.md` §10.

**What it must do:**
- **Opening:** staff open it and pick the **Branch** the tablet is at (351). Today's Sessions and name search cover that Branch. Members of other Branches follow the "check in at other Branches" rule (§3.5).
- **Locked:** only QR, personal code (§3.2) and name search work (317). Leaving needs a **staff member's own kiosk code**; the log records who unlocked it. There is no shared School passcode (320).
- **Check-in:** after code or name, a welcome screen shows the photo, name, membership status and today's classes to tick, then **Confirm**. Every check-in goes through the School's check-in rules (§3.5), with the refusal reason shown. A Class Pack with no credits offers the same pack, and check-in completes once paid (Decision 316).
- **What the tablet shows about people (351):**
  - name search: first name, last-name initial, photo, and Member or Visitor;
  - a child's photo only while the Guardian's camera consent is active (Decisions 57, 69);
  - Recent Check-ins: today only, first name and last-name initial, the last 10.
- **Sign-up tab:** the School's front-desk sign-up form (Decision 318), Visitor or Member (315). The check-in code is generated and shown read-only; the join date is today. A sign-up made here **waits for staff approval**. A child is signed up only by their Guardian, with consent.
- **Find Me:** only says "you're already a member" and sends the person to Check-in. It never shows or fills in stored details (351).

**Suggested build:**
- **A kiosk session token** scoped to one School and Branch, with no access beyond the kiosk endpoints. It's issued when staff open the mode and recorded with who opened it. It must not carry the opening staff member's own rights; the tablet stays signed in while unattended.
- **Kiosk endpoints:**
  - today's classes at the Branch;
  - name search returning only the fields above;
  - check in by personal code;
  - check in a chosen member to chosen classes;
  - recent check-ins;
  - submit a sign-up for approval;
  - unlock with a staff code.
- **Rate limits:** on code entry and name search, per kiosk; log failures (as §3.2).

**Done when:**
- A tablet in kiosk mode can check members in by code or name and take sign-ups.
- It can't reach any other part of the portal.
- It shows only the fields above.
- It leaves the mode only with a staff code, recorded in the log.

**Needs first:** §3.1 (Visitors), §3.2 (personal code), §3.5 (check-in rules), the Access page (Decision 320) and sign-up forms (Decision 318).
