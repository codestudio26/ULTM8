How each pass works

01

Fan out

Several agents review the same section in parallel, each with a different adversarial lens — data model, security, feasibility, cross-doc consistency. No agent sees the others' work.

02

Triage

Findings get sorted into two piles: plain bugs and stale cross-references I fix outright, versus real product or architecture calls that need your judgment.

03

Decide

You answer the calls that need a human — I bring a recommendation and the reasoning behind it every time, never a bare question.

04

Encode & verify

Every change is written into the live document, schema-validated, rendered to PDF, and checked page by page before it's sent — then this tracker gets updated.
